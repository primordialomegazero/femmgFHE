\section{The Core Idea}

Take two functionally equivalent Boolean circuits. Encrypt inputs with Golden Fibonacci (GF-N) encryption. Wrap in CKKS fully homomorphic encryption as DualGate $\{a,b\}$ pairs. Project onto $\varphi$ and $\psi$ bases. Process through chaos gates. Superpose. Permute randomly. Apply commutative reconstruction --- four statistical operations (mean, geometric mean, harmonic mean, RMS) that produce identical output regardless of input order.

Because reconstruction is order-independent, and both circuits produce identical Boolean outputs, the final distributions are identical. $\text{KS} = 0$ is an algebraic consequence, not a statistical approximation.

\section{Mathematical Foundation}

$\varphi \approx 1.6180339887498948482$ and $\psi \approx -0.6180339887498948482$ are roots of $Y^2 - Y - 1 = 0$.

\begin{align}
\varphi + \psi &= 1 \label{eq:sum}\\
\varphi \cdot \psi &= -1 \label{eq:product}\\
\varphi^2 &= \varphi + 1,\quad \psi^2 = \psi + 1 \label{eq:square}
\end{align}

These are mathematical facts, provable in two lines of algebra. They are not cryptographic assumptions.

\subsection{DualGate Projections}

A DualGate is a pair $\{a, b\}$. Two projections:

\begin{equation}
\varphi(a,b) = a + b \cdot \varphi \quad \text{(Circuit A)}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\psi(a,b) = a + b \cdot \psi \quad \text{(Circuit B)}
\end{equation}

\begin{lemma}[DualGate Projection Identity]
For any $\{a,b\}$:
\begin{equation}
\varphi(a,b) \cdot \psi(a,b) = a^2 + ab - b^2
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
$\varphi \cdot \psi = (a + b\varphi)(a + b\psi) = a^2 + ab(\varphi + \psi) + b^2(\varphi \cdot \psi) = a^2 + ab(1) + b^2(-1) = a^2 + ab - b^2$, by (\ref{eq:sum}) and (\ref{eq:product}).
\end{proof}

\subsection{Commutative Reconstruction}

For a multiset $V = \{v_1, \ldots, v_n\}$, define four commutative statistics:

\begin{align}
\text{AM}(V) &= \frac{1}{n}\sum v_i \quad \text{(Arithmetic Mean)}\\
\text{GM}(V) &= \left(\prod(v_i + \varepsilon)\right)^{1/n} \quad \text{(Geometric Mean)}\\
\text{HM}(V) &= n \left/ \sum \frac{1}{v_i + \delta} \right. \quad \text{(Harmonic Mean)}\\
\text{RMS}(V) &= \sqrt{\frac{1}{n}\sum v_i^2} \quad \text{(Root Mean Square)}
\end{align}

The reconstruction is a linear combination:

\begin{equation}
\text{reconstruct}(V) = 0.35 \cdot \text{AM}(V) + 0.25 \cdot \text{GM}(V) + 0.25 \cdot \text{HM}(V) + 0.15 \cdot \text{RMS}(V)
\end{equation}

All four component operations are commutative. Therefore, for any permutation $\sigma$: $\text{reconstruct}(\sigma(V)) = \text{reconstruct}(V)$. The output is independent of the random permutation applied during Fractal Transform.
